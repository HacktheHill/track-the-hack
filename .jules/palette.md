## 2026-09-25 - Add type="search" to search inputs
**Learning:** For optimal UX across mobile devices and modern desktop browsers, explicitly specifying `type="search"` on search-purpose inputs ensures the correct virtual keyboard (often showing a "search" or "go" button) is presented, and often gives access to built-in browser "clear field" buttons.
**Action:** Always check the intended use of a text input, and use `type="search"` when filtering or searching content.
