## 2024-05-18 - Promise.all in tRPC Routers\n**Learning:** When writing Next.js/tRPC endpoints that aggregate multiple metrics or independent dashboard data points, a sequence of independent Prisma queries (`await prisma.count()`, etc.) executed sequentially acts as a massive bottleneck, turning endpoint latency into `O(sum of queries)`.\n**Action:** Always group independent Prisma database queries in tRPC routers within a `Promise.all` block. Additionally, review endpoints to reuse variables for data fetched once, eliminating redundant queries.
